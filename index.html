build Do you charge to fit the trackers?

No. Fitting is included in the licence. Dashcams and devices (tablets, cradles or phones) are optional extras, quoted separately if you want them.

school What training do we get?

A dedicated implementation consultant, the same person throughout, with around 15 hours of one-to-one time over Teams. They build the system around your process and import your data. You also get a dedicated account manager.

contract Are we tied into a contract?

The standard term is 36 months. That’s why the implementation cost is spread across it at £133 a month rather than charged upfront.

support_agent Who do we call when something goes wrong?

Our support team, 24/7, by phone, email or live chat from the app. Everyone is UK-based, working out of our Leeds office.
